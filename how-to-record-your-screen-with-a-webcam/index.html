How to record your screen with a webcam

Free Screen Recorder supports an optional Webcam PiP (picture-in-picture) overlay on top of your screen capture—useful for tutorials and demos.

Enable webcam PiP

  1. Start screen capture with Start Screen + Mic.
  2. Click Webcam PiP and allow camera access when prompted.
  3. A floating camera window appears over the preview. Position yourself and verify lighting.
  4. Record clips as usual with Start Clip.

Sync microphone audio

Camera previews can lag behind audio. Use Mic offset (ms) on the right panel (default 120ms) and click Set while capture is idle to line up voice with the PiP feed.

Limits

PiP and dual capture are demanding. Close unused tabs. On phones, camera + screen capture may be unsupported—use a desktop Chromium browser for reliable results.

Return to the recorder when you are ready.
